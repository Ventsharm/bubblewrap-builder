Ventsharm Android App Builder

Turn your website into a signed Android APK and Play Store AAB. PWAs use Bubblewrap automatically; ordinary websites fall back to WebView.

1. Your app

Enter the information for the app you want to create.

Must start with https://. Service worker found = Bubblewrap. No service worker = WebView. If the manifest is missing but a service worker exists, the builder creates the manifest automatically.
Use lowercase letters with dots. Keep the same package name when updating an existing Play Store app.
This opens the standard Files picker instead of Google Photos. Choose a PNG, JPG, JPEG or WEBP image from Downloads, Images or internal storage. It will be resized to 512 × 512 before the build starts.

These values control the Android launch screen and app window.

3. Version
